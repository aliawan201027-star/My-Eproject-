04 — THE SMART ADVENTURE

THE BEST SUMMIT IS A SAFE RETURN

Adventure
with awareness.

Mountains are beautiful, powerful and unpredictable. Build your skills gradually, check forecasts and local advisories, and never underestimate altitude or changing weather.

  • ✓ Plan your route and tell someone your itinerary.
  • ✓ Carry suitable layers, navigation and emergency supplies.
  • ✓ Go with trained leaders when skills or terrain demand it.
  • ✓ Turn back when conditions or your team say so.
